# Registration and Auth0 contact completion — 3 November 2026

User requires Vietnamese phone at ordinary registration and email/phone completion for Auth0. User explicitly clarified legacy blank phones may be filled with generated format-correct values, without possession verification or a supplied mapping. Never call those values actual account-owner numbers.

- Shared backend normalization and frontend checks accept Vietnamese mobile domestic 10 digits (`03/05/07/08/09`) or landline 11 digits (`02`), and equivalent `+84`; spaces/dots/parentheses/hyphens supported. Reject foreign prefixes, legacy 11-digit mobile numbers, wrong lengths, letters and non-ASCII digits. This checks format, not allocation/ownership. Registration and phone updates require nonblank phone. No SMS or automatic `phoneVerified`.
- V51 adds nullable `auth_accounts.contact_details_confirmed_at`, separate from identity evidence. Blank-only legacy backfill assigns deterministic `+849` + eight account-ID digits, resets phone verification, increments version once, revokes the affected accounts' prior sessions and invalidates pending email challenges. Existing nonblank contacts, credentials/roles/status/verification, identity evidence and contract snapshots stay unchanged. Unverified numbers do not reserve a unique identity. Migration is idempotent and must not be a fixture reset.
- Auth0 login without required contact returns HTTP428 before any internal session/account creation. Same validated evidence may be resubmitted with email/phone, never client-submitted citizen proof. Existing bindings lock/refresh account, require matching stored email, normalize phone, persist confirmation/evidence before bulk session revocation clears persistence context. Changed phone revokes old sessions and pending email challenges; the new session is then created. Confirmed contacts cannot be changed via login. Locked/inactive accounts remain blocked; unique subject/CCCD and no-auto-link rules remain.
- New accounts may collect email even if an eventual username-only provider omits its email claim; email possession is not inferred. Existing accounts change email only via the established OTP settings flow. New contact completion never changes role, passwords, source evidence or saved snapshots.
- Google quick registration also requires phone before creating a new account/session; HTTP428 `GOOGLE_PHONE_REQUIRED` requests it, then the same Google credential is resubmitted with phone. Email comes from the validated Google token; existing subject lookup/no-auto-link and returning-account behavior remain. This closes an alternate signup path around the ordinary form's phone requirement.
- Auth0 CCCD login uses database username/identifier configuration and exact-user username PATCHes. Credentials remain with Auth0; username is not trusted as legal identity. See setup/auth0-cccd-login.md. No provider change is implied by preparing patches.

V50 is reserved by the independent multiple-role branch; V51 is independent of that work. When merging, preserve both migrations and role behavior rather than replacing one branch's account/controller changes with the other.
